# Nika-maskot basert på brukerens referansebilder

Laget 15. oktober 2026 med den innebygde image_gen-funksjonen (ikke CLI). Fire brukerleverte fotografier ble brukt som identitetsreferanser, ikke som redigeringsmål. Originalfotoene er ikke en del av repoet eller publiseringen.

Publisert ressurs: `public/assets/nika-dog-reference-v2.webp`, 930 × 1020 piksler, ekte alfakanal. Generert PNG er skalert og WebP-komprimert uten å endre motivet. Den eldre SVG-filen er bevart. Brukes som Nika-maskot i registrering, dashboardbanner og profilkort.

## Endelig prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent-background dog mascot illustration for the existing Norwegian Nika pet-care web app.
Input images: all four supplied photos are SUBJECT / IDENTITY REFERENCE images of the same dog, not edit targets. Use them together to reproduce this individual dog's distinctive appearance. Do not reproduce any of their scenery.
Primary request: a polished, warm, gently dimensional illustrated mascot of THIS small long-haired black, tan and cream dog, replacing a generic golden dog. Preserve its recognizable coloring and proportions: mostly black silky fluffy coat, upright triangular ears with long wispy fringes, small tan patches above both eyes, tan cheeks and lower legs/paws, pale cream-white muzzle/chin and generous chest ruff, dark brown expressive almond-shaped eyes, small black nose, and a large curled plumed black tail with pale cream fringe. Use the photos for exact markings. A simple red harness and a small plain heart-shaped tag echo the references; no writing on tag, no leash.
Style/medium: premium Scandinavian family-friendly soft 3D illustration, tasteful sculpted volume, subtle fur detail and warm soft studio light. Recognizably the referenced dog, not a generic Pomeranian, not a golden retriever, not photorealism, not an emoji or a flat icon. Natural alert gentle expression; slight friendly smile, restrained stylization without oversized baby eyes or round bear ears.
Composition: ONE dog only, full body sitting comfortably, front three-quarter view toward the viewer. Both ears, paws and curled fluffy tail fully visible. Balanced near-portrait composition suitable for a 620-by-680 CSS hero illustration, compact silhouette with a small clean margin around every edge. No text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no extra props.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with alpha. No solid white/cream backdrop, no checkerboard drawn into image, no playground/buildings/ground from references. At most a very subtle soft contact shadow. Keep the dog dark black with readable soft highlights and faithful tan/cream markings; do not recolor it to match a green palette.
```
